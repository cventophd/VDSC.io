# VDSC Policies 

1. VDSC standard rates for academic collaboration includes co-authorship, as the publication value supports the professional development of its members. If this project is a work-for-hire agreement where the member will not receive authorship, VDSC rate is increased by 50% to account for the transfer of intellectual property without professional credit.
2. No Data Analysis without a written and signed invoice. No exceptions.
3. Initial contact email to VDSC must be made by the project PI to chrisventodatascience@gmail.com, please do not email any of the LLC members directly with their personal emails.
4. All projects required a sign letter by the project PI authorizing VDSC to conduct the analysis. Projects with multiple PIs or Co-PIs will be required to provide a written letter from all aforementioned individuals.
5. IP protected datasets require authorization from the specific issuing office and all project PIs and managers.
6. HSRB and other types of identifiable data require a copy of the IRB or data sharing agreement.
7. All project code will be uploaded and shared by Github and publicized.
8. Data Analysis that require cloud computing resources, or (SaaS) require either (1) an advancement payment prior to project analysis or (2) payment directly by the commissioning lab.
9. VDSC reserve the right to refuse or terminate any data analysis project at any time in cases where analysis can be determined impractical or unsafe to do so. Client may still be subjected to any charges.
10. Client has the right to terminate affiliation with VDSC, but maybe still liable for charges for any rendered services
11. VDSC does not grantee that any statistically results or publishable results. Obviously, VDSC works to do their best and provide the best analysis possible, but we cannot always grantee the final analysis will be publishable.
12. All payments are done through Mercury and payable to Vento Data Science Consulting LLC. No payments should be given directly to any member of the LLC. No cash payments are accepted. Only digital US dollar payments will be accepted. All payments given improperly will voided and return to sender. Client is responsible for any payment surge charges or processing fees.
13. Meetings scheduled 48 hours or more in advanced will be charged the standard business rate. Any meetings that need to be scheduled less then 48 hours in advanced without prior authorization or notification will be billed with the "emergency meeting" rate. Meetings canceled less than 24 hours in advance will incur a 50% fee of the agreed-upon rate. No-shows will be billed at 75% of the meeting rate. Exceptions may be made at the company's sole discretion for documented emergencies, sudden illnesses, or severe weather events.
14. VDSC protects the absolute confidentiality of its clients, proprietary projects, and associated services. In turn, clients are required to maintain reciprocal confidentiality regarding all correspondence, data, and analyses provided by VDSC.
